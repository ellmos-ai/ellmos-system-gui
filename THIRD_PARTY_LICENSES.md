## Control icons (`src/lib/ui-icons.mjs`)

The 22 control icons were generated by agy (the model that ran is not recorded). A path comparison against Feather 4.29.2
and lucide-static 1.70.0 showed that most of them reproduce or follow Feather and Lucide icons, some with identical
geometry. They are attributed here. The per-icon source is also recorded in `ICON_SOURCES` in `src/lib/ui-icons.mjs`;
`tests/ui-icons.test.mjs` checks that every icon has a source and every named library has an entry in this file.
