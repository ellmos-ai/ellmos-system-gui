# Third-party licenses

This file lists material in `ellmos-system-gui` that comes from other projects, with the license notices their licenses
require. The project's own license is in `LICENSE`.

## Icons

Two groups of line icons are covered here.

- **Control icons** (`src/lib/ui-icons.mjs`, 22 icons): generated by agy; the model that ran is not recorded.
- **Shell icons** (`src/components/Header.astro` `LINE_ICON_SVGS`, the inline header glyphs and theme buttons, and
  `src/lib/domain-pins.mjs` `DOMAIN_SVG_PATHS`, 59 icons): written before PB-5b; their origin was never recorded.

Method: every icon was compared with the icons of the same meaning in feather-icons 4.29.2, lucide-static 1.70.0 and
lucide-static 0.263.0 (path and shape comparison). Identical shapes or numbers are "identical geometry"; a number overlap
of 0.7 or more, or one identical shape in a clearly related drawing, is "derived"; 0.45 to 0.7 is "similar" and is
attributed as a precaution. Below that the icon is listed as own. This is a comparison against those three versions only,
not a statement about all icon sets. The source of every icon is recorded in `ICON_SOURCES` (`ui-icons.mjs`) and
`SHELL_ICON_SOURCES` (`shell-icon-sources.mjs`); `tests/ui-icons.test.mjs` checks that every icon has a source and that
every named library has a license entry here that lists the icon.
